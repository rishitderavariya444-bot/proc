# Procuro website: photo list

Every photo slot on the site, grouped into folders. Put each photo at the path shown, with **exactly** that file name, and I'll wire them in without any back-and-forth.

## How to deliver them

1. Make the folders below on your computer, inside one folder called `photos`.
2. Name each file exactly as listed (lower case, hyphens, `.jpg`). PNG or WebP is fine too; just keep the name the same.
3. Upload to GitHub: open the repository, go to `src/assets/`, then **Add file → Upload files**, and drag the whole `photos` folder in. Click **Commit changes**. (Your repository has one branch, so the photos land in the right place automatically.)
   - GitHub's web upload takes up to 100 files at a time, so upload one folder per go if needed.
4. Tell me "photos uploaded". I'll resize and compress them automatically, so send the largest originals you have.

Missing photos are fine: any slot without a file keeps its grey "Photo to come" box.

## Key

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Stock OK** | A free Unsplash photo can stand in until you have your own (Stage 8.5) |
| **Real only** | Must be your own photo. Stock here would misrepresent your stock, people or offices |

**Sizes:** landscape photos at least 2400 px wide for the large ones and 1600 px for the rest; portraits at least 1200 × 1500 px. Phone photos are fine if they're sharp and well lit.
